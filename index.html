How it plays

Nobody on the block gets hit

The people in need stand along the street. The strike button helps them. The villains still swing, and they still go down.

01

Help the neighbors

Walk up to anyone with a need bubble and press J. A meal, care, a RENT FROZEN notice on their building, a good job. They light up and head home.

←→↑↓J
02

Stop the machine

Cruz and Greene roam the streets; a boss waits at the end of each one. Light, heavy, special, grab, throw. Pick up a pipe or a bottle. Double-tap to run or back off.

JKLGSpace
03

Keep the witness standing

Help the witness and she follows you with a folder of receipts. Get her to the boss on her feet and the boss starts a third down.
