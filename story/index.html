What they’re running on

Campaign proposals from the sites they ran on: Zohran for NYC, Abdul for Senate and Talarico for Texas. Where something has already passed, stalled or been blocked, the card says so.

Mamdani

Freeze the rent

Freeze the rent for every stabilized tenant. The Rent Guidelines Board voted a freeze in June 2026; landlords are suing to undo it.

src 40, 33
Mamdani

Fast, fare-free buses

End the fare on every city bus and speed them up with priority lanes, queue-jump signals and loading zones. Albany has not funded it yet.

src 40, 100
Mamdani

Community safety

A Department of Community Safety: mental-health crisis response, outreach workers in 100 subway stations, gun-violence programs, and an 800 percent increase for hate-violence prevention.

src 40
Mamdani

No-cost child care

Free child care from six weeks to five years, with child-care workers paid like public-school teachers. The first 2-K seats opened in September 2026.

src 40, 34
Mamdani

City-owned groceries

City-owned stores with no rent and no property tax, selling near wholesale. The first site, La Marqueta in East Harlem, is projected to open in 2027.

src 40, 35
Mamdani

200,000 homes

Triple production of permanently affordable, union-built, rent-stabilized homes: 200,000 over ten years.

src 40
Mamdani

Bad landlords

One office for code enforcement. If the owner will not make the repair, the city does it and sends the bill.

src 40
Mamdani

Who pays

Match New Jersey's 11.5 percent corporate tax and add 2 percent on income over $1 million. The governor blocked the millionaires tax in 2026.

src 40, 101
Mamdani

$30 by 2030

Raise New York City's minimum wage to $30 an hour by 2030.

src 46
El-Sayed

Medicare for All

Medicare for every American, including vision, dental and hearing. No premiums, copays or deductibles. Medical debt abolished.

src 42
El-Sayed

Money out of politics

No corporate PAC money. Overturn Citizens United. Ban outside spending by super PACs and dark-money groups. Public election funding.

src 43, 114
El-Sayed

Put money in your pocket

Tax capital gains over $1 million like wages, close the stepped-up basis loophole, and exempt the first $50,000 of income from federal tax.

src 44
El-Sayed

Housing

A building boom and a federal renter bill of rights. Stop big corporations from speculating on homes and ban rent-fixing software.

src 44
El-Sayed

Clean water

Water bills capped at 2 percent of household income, no shutoffs for families who cannot pay, and every lead pipe in Michigan out by 2030.

src 43
El-Sayed

Schools

Universal child care and pre-K, a $60,000 minimum teacher salary, and a tuition-free path through college or an apprenticeship.

src 44
El-Sayed

Data centers

Data centers pay for their own power and water so household bills do not rise, and neighbors get a say.

src 45
Talarico

Public schools

No private-school vouchers. Universal pre-K for 3- and 4-year-olds, more teachers, more counselors, and phonics-based reading help.

src 67
Talarico

Raise pay

Raise the minimum wage, expand overtime, and pass paid family leave.

src 68, 71
Talarico

Money out

Ban super PACs and corporate PACs, ban partisan gerrymandering, and ban members of Congress from trading individual stocks.

src 69
Talarico

Lower costs

A public health-insurance option, caps on insulin and drug prices, medical-debt relief, and down-payment help for first-time buyers.

src 70, 71
Talarico

Child care

Universal child care, paid family leave and a bigger Child Tax Credit: his New American Dream agenda from August 2026.

src 71
Ocasio-Cortez

Green New Deal

A ten-year national mobilization: union jobs repairing infrastructure, cutting carbon, and tying climate policy to economic and racial justice. Introduced as a resolution in 2019, not as enacted law.

src 150, 156
Ocasio-Cortez

Medicare for All

A national single-payer health program. She has argued it would lower overall health-care costs.

src 154
Ocasio-Cortez

Housing

Housing as a right in NY-14, including for families facing eviction and rising rents in the Bronx and Queens.

src 154
Ocasio-Cortez

Workers

Dignity for bartenders, retail workers and bus drivers. She has refused corporate PAC money since the 2018 campaign.
