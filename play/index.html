Capitol
Crashers

Two fighters. Three blocks. The Capitol at the end of the street.

  • Arrows or WASD move. Double-tap forward to run, then J to strike. Double-tap back to retreat.
  • J light combo, K heavy, L special, Space jump. Hold forward and K to lunge. Hold back and K to reverse. J as you get up.
  • Up close, J grabs. Mash J to hit them, then K to throw. Hold back and K to throw behind you. Mash J if they grab you.
  • Walk over a pipe or bottle to take it. J swings a pipe. K throws it.
