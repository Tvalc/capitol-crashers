Capitol
Crashers

Two fighters. A block that needs them. The Landlord at the end of the street.

  • Neighbors in need stand along the street. Walk up and press J to help: a meal, care, a rent freeze, a job. Nobody gets hit.
  • A witness with receipts will follow you. Keep them standing and they put a third of the boss on the record.
  • Arrows or WASD move. Double-tap forward to run, then J to strike a villain. Double-tap back to retreat.
  • J light combo, K heavy, L special, Space jump. Hold forward and K to lunge. Hold back and K to reverse. J as you get up.
  • Press G to grab a nearby enemy. J punches; walking never grabs. K throws. Hold back and K to throw behind you.
  • Walk over a pipe or bottle to take it. J swings a pipe. K throws it.
